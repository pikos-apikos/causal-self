# Contributing

This repository is a working research note, not a finished theory. The most
valuable contributions are those that make the hypothesis easier to falsify.

Useful contributions include:

- counterexamples and alternative explanations;
- clearer operational definitions;
- stronger null hypotheses;
- experimental designs that separate causal self-modeling from arbitrary
  anchoring, additional memory, or additional compute;
- corrections to mathematical or scientific claims;
- relevant primary literature, with a short explanation of the connection.

Please open an issue before proposing a large rewrite. Keep claims proportional
to evidence and distinguish among:

- established result;
- interpretation of prior work;
- working hypothesis;
- experimental prediction;
- philosophical speculation.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the
repository's CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
